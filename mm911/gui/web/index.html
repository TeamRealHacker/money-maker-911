How many forms?

Smallest to Final Form. You can change it any time.

Optional. Photos of you, your friends, memes, anything. Smallest first; the number of pictures sets the forms.

Drop your sound

Use any clip, like one from Myinstants. It's pitched up automatically on every merge.

Tip: plain pops and boings are safest from copyright claims.
